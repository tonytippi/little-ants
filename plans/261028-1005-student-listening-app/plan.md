# Student listening app (Listening Time) with schools, enrollments and parent accounts

Children practise at home with the unit's songs, chants, rhymes, stories and word cards: one "leaf" (a 6–15 minute
playlist) a day, 40 leaves per unit, as described in the program's Listening Time Programme Guide
(`00_PROGRAM/05_Listening-Time` on Drive) and the U05 prototype (`U05_5_Listening-App/index.html`). Parents sign in
with Google and see their children. Teachers use the same app "At school" on the classroom screen and see who listens
at home. To support this, the organisation model becomes School → Campus → Class, with children who move between
classes and schools.

## Decisions (from the user, 2026-10-27/28)

- Leaves are generated by the guide's rules for every unit at publish time (no hand-written plans for now).
- Parents are separate from staff: own tables, own Google sign-in, own sessions. Not in `user`.
- A parent is linked to children by the email the school enters. Parents cannot self-register.
- At most 2 signed-in devices per parent.
- Access: while a child has an active enrollment anywhere, the child can open every unit they have studied plus the
  current one. When the child leaves (no active enrollment), access stops immediately.
- Several schools; each school has campuses; each campus has classes. A class runs one unit at a time and moves on
  to the next unit; children may leave, classes may be merged or stopped. A child can continue at another
  campus/school.
- A new school sees the child's listening progress and studied units, not other schools' teacher observations.
- At school mode lives in the student app. Domain: `la-student.passionedu.org`.
- Approach follows the sibling project `anhhoa` (Google OIDC for parents, `ParentProfile` + `StudentParent`), with
  database sessions instead of stateless cookies so the device limit and immediate lock-out work.

## Outcome and acceptance

- HQ admins manage schools; school admins manage their campuses, classes, children and parent emails; teachers see
  only their classes. A class shows its unit history; admins can start the next unit, move a child, merge or stop a
  class.
- A parent whose email was entered signs in with Google, sees their children and, per child, the studied units.
  A third device is refused until one is signed out. A child who left is locked at the next request.
- Each published unit has 40 leaves that follow the guide's rules (only known materials, bridge song, balance,
  party/favourites leaves), checked by tests.
- At home: the yellow leaf plays from start to end (recordings, or the device voice where there is none), the leaf
  turns green, at most 2 new leaves a day, 4-leaves-a-week goal, Library, My listening. Works offline after
  downloading the unit; progress syncs when online.
- At school: a teacher picks the lesson's leaf on the big screen and sees the session plan.
- The Teacher app shows each child's green leaves per week and whether 80% of the class reached 4.
- `seed-test` creates a test school, campus Mio Lingo, the Unit 5 class, children and test parents.

Non-goals: payment and licence sales, parent sign-in other than Google, push notifications, hand-edited leaf plans,
recording audio, a Vietnamese translation of the whole UI (parent-facing instructions are bilingual).

## Risks

- Google sign-in inside an installed iOS home-screen app may finish in Safari instead of the app (separate cookie
  stores). Test on an iPad early (phase 3); fallback is opening sign-in in the same window, then an email code if it
  still fails.
- The migration is a clean break of the organisation tables. Acceptable because all current data is test data
  (re-created by `seed-test`); the database is backed up first.
- Device voices differ by phone; rhymes and stories read by the device voice may sound poor until recordings exist.
- The release payload gains the leaf plan; old releases have none, so units must be republished (seed-test does it).
- Child data (names, progress) of minors: only the display name and listening log are stored; consent date stays on
  the child.

## Rollback

Each phase is its own commit. Before phase 1's migration, `pnpm --filter @little-ants/db backup before-schools`;
restore with `restore.sh` as in `docs/operations/deploy-and-restore.md`, and redeploy the previous image tag.
